# cs460student

Welcome to UMatrixB an interactive experience that visits the campus of UMASS
Boston through means of a UI and panoramic photos !!

Once you visit the site, you are met with an STL file of the UMB mascot the 
Beacon encapsulated in a matrix of cubes. Using your mouse you can rotate and 
zoom to inspect the scene as much as you want 

You can further explore by pressing 'e' in which a UI containing all the 
panaoramic photos will reside. Select a photo and you will be automatically
emersed in it. Using the mouse you can pan around and inspect the enviornment 
with the matrix of cubes surrounding your perspective. Further enforcing the 
UMatrixB concept. 

You can select "e" again to jump around from scenes to scenes. Or press
"esc" to return to the STL scene

In terms of interchangability, if you wanted to persona;;ize the enviornment
with your own photos, you can just edit the script where it says "pano_.jpg" 
and swap in any photos of your own. Hope you enjoy the experience.
